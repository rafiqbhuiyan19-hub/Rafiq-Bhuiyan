Project 01 · E-commerce
Meta AdsSalesFB + IG

Challenge

A wide-catalogue store needed sales at a cost low enough to stay profitable on every order.

Strategy

Pixel and Conversions API first. Each product got its own test budget; losers were cut fast, winners got more budget and fresh creative.
